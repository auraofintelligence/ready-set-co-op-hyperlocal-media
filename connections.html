Check trust and sources

Before anything goes public.

A generic checklist cannot create publishing authority. If Ready SET Co-op forms, its members and elected leadership would set its publishing rules together, within the laws and Acts that apply.

Trust Hub boundary notesThe current public notes about what the proposal does and does not claim. Screen Network sourcesStatus labels, source projects and straight answers about what is not agreed. Documentary boundariesA public/private review before film notes or interview material are shared. Community LedgerA public trail for support and acknowledgement when there is real evidence to record.
