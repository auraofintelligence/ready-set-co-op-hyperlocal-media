A public idea for Minjerribah

Local stories. Useful skills. More ways to help.

Ready SET Media joins up local storytelling, beginner-friendly media training and practical project work. The aim is simple: help people share what is happening, learn by doing and find the next useful job.

An invitation to talk, not a done deal.

The whole idea in six steps

Start with something useful.

No giant platform is needed. One real story, one willing group or one small community job is enough to learn what helps.

1Bring a need

A story, notice, event, project or practical problem.

2Check it

Facts, dates, sources, safety and permission.

3Make it

Words, photos, sound, video or a simple public page.

4Share it

Use the channel that suits the people who need it.

5Learn from it

Show someone else how the useful part was done.

6Grow carefully

Repeat work may become training or paid roles when the proper support exists.

Share with care

A good story is not worth breaking trust.

Minjerribah is Quandamooka Country. Cultural stories, language, identity, young people, private details and community knowledge need the right permission and context. A media tool cannot decide that on its own.

AI may help organise or reformat a draft. People remain responsible for the facts, the tone, the permission and the final decision to publish.
