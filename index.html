One network, many ways to reach people

Mixed media should move with the story.

Not every update needs a film. Not every interview needs an article. The production team chooses the form that fits the people, place and moment.

Watch

Phone reports, interviews, explainers, event coverage, live production and longer local films.

Listen

Read

Useful articles, public-source links, transcripts, guides, corrections and project records.

Live

Events and community broadcasts

Sport, meetings, performances, screenings and live updates prepared for the right audience.

Screens

Information where people already are

Simple notices and media for community venues, clubs, shops and future public displays.

Make

Practical learning inside real work

Research, filming, sound, editing, captions, presenting, publishing and media operations.

Human-in-the-loop at every step

Agents carry the load. People hold the responsibility.

No agent publishes by itself. Each step produces a clear recommendation or draft for a person to accept, change, return or stop.

01CaptureAgent organises the contribution. Person confirms what was meant.
02TriageAgent suggests the path. Producer chooses priority and owner.
03VerifyAgent finds sources and conflicts. Person checks the evidence.
04MakeAgent prepares options. Creator shapes the real work.
05ReviewAgent runs checks. Responsible people approve the release.
06PublishAgent prepares each channel. Publisher releases it.
07Follow throughAgent watches for changes. Person corrects, updates or archives.

A human decision is recorded at every hand-off.
