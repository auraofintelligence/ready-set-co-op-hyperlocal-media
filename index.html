The proposal in brief

More than a news website.

The source media program described a lean newsroom, automated onboarding, public story submissions, moderation, multi-channel publishing and work pathways. This version makes those pieces visible without pretending they already exist.

01 · Newsroom

One place to bring local information.

Story tips, club results, event details, business updates, interviews, public-source alerts and corrections enter one visible editorial flow.

See the six-stage workflow
02 · Welcome

A useful first route for different people.

Residents, clubs, businesses, creatives, learners and long-stay visitors choose what they need and are sent to the right public tool or next step.

Try the onboarding prototype
03 · Training

Learn on a real production task.

Research, phone filming, sound, captions, editing, publishing and archive work become small supervised roles instead of one giant course.

See the role pathway
04 · Distribution

Make once, prepare for the right channel.

A checked source can become an article, short video, audio update, newsletter item or community-screen slide without losing its status trail.

A possible coverage desk

Useful beats, not endless content.

Coverage would be selected by the people who eventually govern the outlet. These categories simply show the practical range imagined in the source program.

Local deskNews, consultations, transport and service updates
Culture deskArts, screen, music, exhibitions and local history
Community deskClubs, sport, volunteers, notices and events
Practical deskWeather, beach information, visitor guidance and explainers
Learning deskHow-to clips, media practice and digital confidence
Projects deskWhat changed, who did the work and what happens next

The wider public pitch

Connected to the Minjerribah Screen & Media Network.

The newer network joins the newsroom idea to film, training, noticeboards, screens, possible spaces and a public source trail. Ready SET Co-op Media focuses on the employment, training, onboarding and production system inside that wider picture.

Open the network ↗ Check its sources ↗
Publishing authority If Ready SET Co-op forms, its members and elected leadership would set the publishing rules together, within the laws and Acts that apply.

This website and Luke do not get to decide those rules in advance.
